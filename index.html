Safety Checklists

Complete before fault-finding or mechanical work

Identify the machine and confirm the correct asset.
Review workplace procedures and manufacturer's instructions.
Identify stored energy and potential hazards.
Confirm the machine is in a safe condition before inspection.
Check hydraulic pressure has been safely relieved where applicable.
Secure or support suspended loads and moving components.
Wear the required PPE for the task.
Keep clear of pinch points, rotating parts and stored energy.
Use correct tools and safe inspection methods.
Stop and escalate if the task is outside your training or authorisation.
⚡ Electrical safety

Electrical testing, isolation, wiring, controls and electrical repairs must be performed by a suitably qualified electrician. MachineMate only provides visual/observational guidance for electrical-related symptoms.
