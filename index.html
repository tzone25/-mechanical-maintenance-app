💧 Hydraulic Troubleshooting

Use this as a troubleshooting guide. Always follow site isolation, pressure-release and WHS procedures.

No Hydraulic Pressure

Check:
  1. Oil level and condition
  2. Pump drive / coupling
  3. Pump rotation
  4. Inlet restriction
  5. Relief valve setting
  6. Open-centre / closed-centre configuration
  7. Valve position
  8. Major internal leakage

Low Pressure

Possible causes:
  • Relief valve incorrectly adjusted
  • Worn pump
  • Excessive internal leakage
  • Faulty pressure-control valve
  • Incorrect pump control setting
  • Restricted or damaged components

Hydraulic Overheating

Possible causes:
  • Relief valve bypassing continuously
  • Incorrect pressure setting
  • Restricted cooler
  • Low oil level
  • Incorrect oil viscosity
  • Excessive internal leakage
